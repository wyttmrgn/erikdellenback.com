Career and experiences

Erik grew up loving the Dr. Seuss book Oh, the Places You’ll Go! This page traces a vocational life of learning that comes from following the Lord. Oh, the places you’ll go!

Employers and clients

  1. The Church of Eleven22

    Ordained Pastor 2025; Pastor of Ministry Partnerships 2026 to present

    Erik works with church leadership to help The Church of Eleven22 steward its God-given national platform as the fastest-growing church in America and the fourth largest in the country. His focus is twofold: nationwide pastoral care through pastor retreats, coaching, and hunting and fishing trips; and civic engagement, helping believers think through faith and the Christian’s role in politics and government.

  2. Florida Family Voice

    Chief Executive Officer and Board Member 2025 to present

    Erik joined Florida Family Voice (FFV) to continue the work he began in the Executive Office of the Governor at the intersection of faith, policy, and government. He leads the vision and strategy of FFV, which serves as Florida’s premier defender of Life, Marriage, Family, and Religious Liberty.

  3. State of Florida, Executive Office of the Governor

    Florida’s first Governor’s Liaison for Faith and Community 2019 to 2025

    In 2019, newly elected Governor Ron DeSantis made faith a priority for the state and appointed Erik to his leadership team as Florida’s first Governor’s Liaison for Faith and Community. Erik created the Governor’s Faith and Community Initiative office and led it for nearly six years, including through Covid. Under his leadership, more than 2,200 faith institutions and 5,600 nonprofit partners mobilized to serve Florida’s most vulnerable, and Florida became the first state to roll out CarePortal statewide. He also helped launch and build the Hope Florida initiative and served as its president in 2025.

  4. For Others

    Founding board member, then interim CEO and President 2018 to 2024

    In 2018, Erik helped Christian musician Chris Tomlin start Angel Armies, now For Others, a nation-leading nonprofit addressing the foster care crisis in the United States. He served first as a founding board member and later as interim CEO and President.

  5. Tim Tebow Foundation

    Founding President and Executive Director 2011 to 2017

    Erik led the Tim Tebow Foundation as its founding President and Executive Director for nearly seven years. Under his leadership, the foundation launched numerous domestic and international ministry programs. These included the Tebow CURE Hospital in the Philippines and Night to Shine, now a worldwide prom for people with special needs. He also built and owned companies with Tim that handled speaking engagements, marketing, business development, and film production.

  6. Flagler College

    Adjunct Professor, Sales in Sports Management 2008 to 2011

    While serving at the Gator Bowl Association, Erik taught sales in Flagler College’s Sports Management program, bringing real-world industry experience into the classroom.

  7. Gator Bowl Association

    Intern to Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer 1999 to 2011

    Erik started in 1999 as the Gator Bowl parade intern and rose through the association over nearly 12 years. Along the way he served as Director of Ticketing, Director of Promotions and the PATCH program, and ultimately Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer. Most notably, when the ACC expanded to 12 teams and added a football championship in 2004, Erik was named the first Director of the ACC Championship. He built and ran the first three championship games as chief liaison to the ACC, Dr Pepper, ABC Sports, and 90 game sponsors.

  8. University of Florida

    University Athletic Association marketing intern Spring 2000

    Erik’s first job in college athletics was as a marketing intern for UF’s University Athletic Association.

Businesses owned

  1. Hat n Hoodie Consulting

    Founder 2017 to present

    Erik founded Hat n Hoodie Consulting (formerly Mercy Seeds Consulting), a national firm serving individuals, businesses, nonprofits, ministries, and families who steward platforms of affluence or influence. More on the Consulting page.

  2. Joey’s Custard

    Independent owner, Sanibel Island 2021 to 2022

    Erik owned Joey’s Custard, a family-run shop on Sanibel Island built on the motto “where our family serves your family.” Hurricane Ian closed it in September 2022.

  3. Auntie Anne’s Pretzels

    Owner and franchisee 2017 to 2024

    Erik owned and operated a co-branded Auntie Anne’s and Planet Smoothie storefront in St. Augustine, Florida. From 2019 to 2021, he also designed, built, and ran an Auntie Anne’s food truck across Northeast Florida.

  4. Planet Smoothie

    Owner and franchisee 2017 to 2024

    Erik owned and operated a co-branded Planet Smoothie and Auntie Anne’s storefront in St. Augustine, Florida.

  5. TD Autographs

    Co-owner 2012 to 2017

    Erik co-owned TD Autographs, a one-stop shop that handled all logistics and sales for Tim Tebow autographs and memorabilia.

  6. TD Speaking

    TD Speaking

    Owner 2012 to 2017

    Erik owned TD Speaking, which booked and managed every Tim Tebow speaking engagement from booking through event day.

Projects

  1. Night to Shine

    Began and implemented at the Tim Tebow Foundation 2014

    Erik started Night to Shine at the Tim Tebow Foundation as a prom night experience for people with special needs. It is now a worldwide movement held in all 50 states and 24 countries, representing more than 32 Christian denominations.

  2. Run the Race

    Executive producer 2016 to 2019

    Erik executive produced the 2019 national theatrical release Run the Race alongside Tim and Robby Tebow. The sports drama highlights brotherhood, resilience, and contrasting worldviews.

  3. He Calls Me Daughter

    Executive producer 2024 to 2026

    Erik executive produced this faith-based documentary, released nationally in 2026 and now seen around the world. The film explores the “father wound” in women and how healing comes through God the Father. It follows the Kendrick Brothers’ Show Me the Father, which addressed the father wound in men.

  4. Haiti United

    Co-founder and host 2024 to present

    Erik co-founded and hosts Haiti United, a movement of 50 organizations, ministries, and leaders who continue to serve in Haiti, united by a love for Jesus and hope for Haiti.
