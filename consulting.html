Hat n Hoodie Consulting

The advice comes from having sat in the chair.

One time someone said to me that I was the Bill Belichick of leadership, and before I had time to feel good about what a compliment that could be, they quickly added that they simply meant I am always either in a suit or a hat and hoodie. That’s when Hat n Hoodie Consulting was born, because that is definitely the outfit I would rather be in.

Since 2017 Erik has advised individuals, for-profits, nonprofits, projects, organizations, ministries and families who carry real influence or affluence and wish to steward it well.

Due to the width and variety of clients over the years, the areas of consulting have varied greatly and include:

  • Fundraising
  • Vision casting
  • Strategy
  • Staffing
  • Culture
  • Philanthropy advice on giving, and holding accountability for that giving
  • Taking an idea or vision to a reality
  • Business building
  • Starting foundations

He has started a major conference football championship, built two national foundations from their founding, run a nation-leading statewide network inside government, led national nonprofits, worked for the fastest growing churches in the country and owned several businesses and restaurant franchises.
