The point of this site

Hi everybody, my name is Erik and I’m so grateful to have you check out my site.

Let me keep this simple… I don’t think I have ever deserved or been fully qualified for any role that God has allowed me to do, but somehow I have been blessed to be a husband to Elizabeth, a Dad to 5 and I have spent the last 25 years getting to lead right next to some very well-known amazing, world-changing people.

Tim Tebow - Chris Tomlin - Governor Ron DeSantis.

These pages share a little bit about those experiences and I hope they are both encouraging to you and honoring and glorifying to the Lord.

If I can ever help in a consulting capacity, or you would like me to speak, please let me know.

Galatians 6:14
