“I’ve had a unique career, spending more than 25 years in the business of football and alongside Tim Tebow, Chris Tomlin, and Governor Ron DeSantis in sports, ministry, and government, respectively. Erik has spoken hundreds of times to groups throughout Florida, around the country, and across the globe.
