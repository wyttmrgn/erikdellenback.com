# Hero video: where each clip came from, and who to ask

The hero loop is cut from six source videos. Copyright in each belongs to whoever recorded and published it, not to the person on screen. Two sources are Florida Family Voice's own channel, which Erik runs, so nothing is needed there. Four need a one-line permission from the publisher. In every case Erik was the invited speaker or guest, so a yes is likely; the point is to have it in writing before launch.

Looked up 12 September 2026 from each video's page and the publisher's website.

## Email drafts (from Erik, from his Florida Family Voice address)

Each one is short on purpose. The three things that make a yes easy: exactly which seconds, that it is muted, and that it comes down on request.

Subject: Permission to use a few seconds of my 2025 conference talk

Hi CarePortal team,

I'm building a personal website (erikdellenback.com) and my hero banner is a short, silent video loop of moments from the last few years. One of them is about five seconds from your recording of my talk at the 2025 CarePortal Conference (youtube.com/watch?v=x2ttImX0CO4, around the 6:38 mark).

May I use it? It plays muted, with a dark overlay and my name over it, on my site only. I'm glad to credit CarePortal in the site notes, and I'll take it down the same day if you ever ask.

A simple "yes" by reply is all I need. Thank you for the conference and for what you do.

Erik Dellenback
