# The other pages: layout plan

Written 14 September 2026. The homepage is settled (video, photo strip, story with the closing call, logo row, footer). This is the plan for everything the menu points to. Same system throughout: navy grounds that alternate, Karla bold caps headings, gold for the one thing to click on each page, logos on a white panel.

The menu has six items. Two are sections of the homepage today (About, Crazy Experiences), two are draft pages (Speaking, Consulting), one is hidden until there is content (Podcast), one is the footer (Contact).

## Speaking (build first: it is where "Invite Erik to speak" lands)

1. **Opening band, navy.** Photo of Erik at a podium on the left (the FFV podium photo, 1800px, is the best we have), and on the right: "INVITE ERIK TO SPEAK" as the page title, one sentence in his voice, and the gold link (email with the subject pre-filled).
2. **What he talks about, slate.** His five phrases as talk titles, each with one line of description. Titles are his; the lines need him.
3. **Rooms he has spoken in, white panel.** A logo row for this page only: Florida Family Voice, CarePortal Conference, The Church of Eleven22, Tim Tebow Foundation, the Florida Children and Youth Cabinet (state seal). All of these appear in the hero video, so they are true.
4. **For programs and press, navy.** The third-person bio (already written), a "download headshot" link once a headshot exists, and the same gold link again.

## Consulting (Mercy Seeds)

1. **Opening band, navy.** "MERCY SEEDS CONSULTING", the two paragraphs already drafted, gold link "Start a conversation".
2. **What he has built, run and owned, white panel.** A logo row instead of the current text list: Tim Tebow Foundation, For Others, Florida Family Voice, the Governor's Faith and Community Initiative seal, Fellowship Adventures, Auntie Anne's, Planet Smoothie, Joey's Custard. The three restaurants are the point here: he has run for-profit businesses, not only ministries. This is where those logos earn their place.
3. **Who it is for, slate.** Three short columns: individuals and families of influence, ministries and nonprofits, businesses. Needs a sentence each from Erik.

## About (new page; the homepage keeps the short story)

1. **Opening band, navy.** A real portrait when one exists (none of the 23 photos is a clean solo portrait). Until then the story text alone.
2. **The whole story, slate.** The homepage paragraphs are the introduction; this page adds a timeline built from the CV: Gator Bowl 2000 to 2011, Tim Tebow Foundation 2011 to 2017, restaurants 2017 to 2024, For Others 2018 to 2024, Governor's office 2019 to 2025, Florida Family Voice 2025 on. One line each, dates in gold.
3. **Boards, honors and callings, white panel.** Logos: The Church of Eleven22, Fellowship Adventures, illumiNations, Florida Blue Key, plus text for Eagle Scout and the Faith and Community Advisory Council.
4. **The films, navy.** Run the Race and He Calls Me Daughter as two poster thumbnails with one line each (the posters Erik sent are small; fine at thumbnail size, and they are the studios' artwork, so they should link out).

## Crazy Experiences (built 14 September 2026 from the Dropbox batch)

The page Erik described in his own notes: photos with a line of text on hover. A masonry grid, each photo with its caption revealed on hover or tap: Bobby Bowden's last game, the python, the sailfish at Casa Vieja, the Auntie Anne's food truck on the beach, the Oval Office prayer, the Tebow Celebrity Golf Classic, Haiti, Night to Shine. Half of these need better originals from Erik; the homepage strip covers the best of what we have today.

## Podcast

Hidden until there are clips. When there are: the Listen grid already in the homepage markup (thumbnail, title, where and how long), linking out to YouTube or Spotify rather than embedding players.

## Contact

The footer is the contact for now: the email, and the menu's Contact item lands there. A page is only worth adding if Erik wants separate routes for speaking, consulting and media, in which case it is three short blocks with three pre-filled mailto links.

## What every page shares

- Top bar with the wordmark and the Menu button; the same slide-in menu; the same footer.
- One gold link per page, never a button.
- A logo row only where the logos are true for that page: the homepage shows the company he has kept, Speaking shows where he has spoken, Consulting shows what he has built and owned, About shows the boards and honors.
