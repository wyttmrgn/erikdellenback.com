# The other pages: layout plan

Written 14 September 2026. The homepage is settled (video, photo strip, story with the closing call, logo row, footer). This is the plan for everything the menu points to. Same system throughout: navy grounds that alternate, Karla bold caps headings, gold for the one thing to click on each page, logos on a white panel.

The menu has six items and, since 17 September 2026, five of them are pages: About, Crazy Experiences, Speaking, Consulting and Contact (a form). Podcast stays hidden until there is content. The homepage logo row leads to a sixth page, In good company.

## What every page shares

- Top bar with the wordmark and the Menu button; the same slide-in menu; the same footer.
- One gold call per page, never a button. The homepage is the exception since 17 September: Erik asked for a second call under the logo row. The contact form's Send is a form control, styled as an outlined gold button, not a link.
- A logo row only where the logos are true for that page: the homepage shows the company he has kept, Speaking shows where he has spoken, Consulting shows what he has built and owned, About shows the boards and honors.
